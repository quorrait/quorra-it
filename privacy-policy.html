Privacy Policy

Effective date: January 1, 2026  ·  Quorra IT LLC

When you submit our contact form we collect your name, company name, email address, and any message you choose to include. We do not collect payment information, and we do not use tracking cookies or analytics scripts on this site. Your information is used solely to respond to your enquiry and, if you become a client, to manage the business relationship. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes. Form submissions are processed by Web3Forms; submitted data is transmitted to their servers to deliver the message to us — please review Web3Forms' own privacy policy for details on how they handle data in transit. Enquiry emails are retained in our inbox for as long as needed to manage the business relationship and deleted when no longer required; if you would like your data removed, email us at support@quorrait.com and we will action it within 30 days. We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information — all data is transmitted over HTTPS, and our email is hosted on Cloudflare with two-factor authentication enforced. You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data we hold about you; contact us at support@quorrait.com to exercise these rights.
