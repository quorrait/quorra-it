Get help

Email support@quorrait.com. Include your company name, your sign-in email, and what you were doing when the problem happened. A screenshot helps. We answer on business days.

Common questions

I can't sign in. Check the company code, your email and your password. The company code is six characters and is shown on your company's TrueFlow website under Mobile App. If it still fails, ask your district manager to reset your password.

How do I get the app? Open your company's TrueFlow website, choose Mobile App, and follow the steps for iPhone or Android.

The app says an update is available. Tap Update (Android) or open the App Store or TestFlight (iPhone) and update. Your saved work stays on the phone.

I don't have a login. TrueFlow accounts are created by your water district. Ask your district manager.

Remove my account or data. Ask your district manager, or email us and we will route the request to the right party. See the privacy policy.
