The short answer

How we got these: pay monthly = 3 months at half price plus 9 months at full price (Enterprise: 3 × $150 + 9 × $300 = $3,150). Pay yearly = the yearly monthly rate × 12, with no first-time discount. There's no setup fee and no contract on either.

We can't work out ServiceTitan's first year because it doesn't publish prices. To compare fairly, ask ServiceTitan to put these six things in writing:

  1. The price per technician per month, and who counts as a technician.
  2. How many office logins are included, and what each extra one costs.
  3. The onboarding, training or implementation fee.
  4. The contract length, and what you owe if you cancel early or your team shrinks.
  5. How much notice you have to give before it auto-renews.
  6. The card and bank processing rates with ServiceTitan Payments, and whether you can use a processor you already have.

Then your first year is: (price per tech × your techs + extra office logins) × 12 + the onboarding fee + any add-ons. For the full cost, multiply the monthly part by the number of months in the contract, not just 12.

Your money goes to your Stripe, not ours

With Quorra Dispatch, card payments go straight into your own Stripe account. We never hold your money. You also log cash and checks just as fast, so the invoice closes and the job moves to Completed on the spot.

ServiceTitan offers ServiceTitan Payments through a payment processing partner, with Tap to Pay, payment links, online payments, membership billing and mobile check capture. It doesn't publish its rates. Its payments FAQ says fees depend on the payment method, the transaction type and your agreement.

If you already have a Stripe account, you keep it. Your payouts, your dashboard, your customer payment records.

How a job runs in Quorra Dispatch

From call to accepted quote

A request from your website form lands in Leads with the customer's name, phone number and what they need, whether it's a no-heat call or a water heater swap. You build the quote from your price book, with a price guide that shows what similar jobs cost in your state. The customer opens a link and accepts.

From booked to paid

After they accept, the customer picks a day and time that fits your hours and your crew, and it lands on your calendar. They get an on-my-way text when your tech is headed over. You log arrival, work and parts as you go, then take card, cash or check on the spot.

All of it runs in one app on your phone. Your techs install the same app and sign in with their own login. It runs on iPhone and Android and also works in the browser.

These are real differences, including things we don't offer. They are not reasons to stay put. Try Quorra Dispatch with your own jobs for 14 days, or talk to us.

  • An existing ServiceTitan contract. Its annual report says annual and multi-year contracts are noncancelable with a minimum fee for the term. Check your order form before you switch; you may owe the rest of the term. You can still start a Dispatch trial and run both for a week.
