About Quorra Dispatch

Quorra Dispatch is made by Quorra IT LLC in Northern California. Brayden Cater owns the company.

What it is

Quorra Dispatch is a phone-first app for home and field service businesses. That includes cleaning, landscaping, pest control, plumbing, HVAC, electrical, and other home and field trades. It is an iPhone and Android app that also works in the browser.

Who it is for

It is for shops from 1 to 15 people. Plans are priced by login: $80, $190, or $300 a month.

How payments work

Card payments go into the contractor's own Stripe account. Quorra does not hold the money.

Support

Email support is included on every plan. Phone support is included on Enterprise. Email support@quorrait.com.
