Questions, answered.

Do I need a card to start?

Yes, you add a card when you sign up, but nothing is charged until your 14-day trial ends. Cancel before then and you pay nothing.

Is there an app for my phone?

Yes. Quorra Dispatch is an app built for your phone, on iPhone and Android. You run the whole business from it, and your crew installs the same app and signs in with their own login.

Where do card payments go?

Straight into your own Stripe account. We don't hold your money.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes, from the Billing page in the app. You keep access until the end of the period you already paid for.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. Upgrade or downgrade in the app and the change is prorated.
