Your money goes to your Stripe, not ours

With Quorra Dispatch, card payments go straight into your own Stripe account. We never hold your money. You also log cash and checks just as fast, so the invoice closes and the job moves to Completed on the spot.

With Housecall Pro, card and bank payments run through Housecall Pro Payments. You connect your business bank account, and Housecall Pro deducts its fee before the deposit lands: 2.59% for cards tapped, swiped or chipped in person, 2.99% when the customer pays an invoice online, 3.49% for cards keyed in, scanned or saved on file and for AmEx and business cards, and 1% for bank payments. Instant payouts cost another 1%.

If you already have a Stripe account, you keep it. Your payouts, your dashboard, your customer payment records.

How a job runs in Quorra Dispatch

From lead to accepted quote

A request from your website form lands in Leads with the customer's name, phone number and what they need. You build the quote with a price guide that shows what similar jobs cost in your state. The customer opens a link and accepts.

From booked to paid

After they accept, the customer picks a day and time that fits your hours and your crew, and it lands on your calendar. They get an on-my-way text when you're headed over. You log arrival, work and parts as you go, then take card, cash or check on the spot.

All of it runs in one app on your phone. Your crew installs the same app and signs in with their own login. It runs on iPhone and Android and also works in the browser.
