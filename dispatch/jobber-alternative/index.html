Your money goes to your Stripe, not ours

With Quorra Dispatch, card payments go straight into your own Stripe account. We never hold your money. You also log cash and checks just as fast, so the invoice closes and the job moves to Completed on the spot.

With Jobber, card payments are processed through Jobber at the rates on its pricing page: 2.9% + 30¢ for online card payments, 2.7% + 30¢ for Tap to Pay, 1% for bank payments, and another 1% if you want instant payouts.

If you already have a Stripe account, you keep it. Your payouts, your dashboard, your customer payment records.

How a job runs in Quorra Dispatch

From lead to accepted quote

A request from your website form lands in Leads with the customer's name, phone number and what they need. You build the quote with a price guide that shows what similar jobs cost in your state. The customer opens a link and accepts.

From booked to paid

After they accept, the customer picks a day and time that fits your hours and your crew, and it lands on your calendar. They get an on-my-way text when you're headed over. You log arrival, work and parts as you go, then take card, cash or check on the spot.

All of it runs in one app on your phone. Your crew installs the same app and signs in with their own login. It runs on iPhone and Android and also works in the browser.

When Jobber is the better pick

We'd rather you pick the right tool than switch twice. Stay on Jobber, or choose it, if:

  • You work alone and only need quotes and invoices. Jobber Core is $29 to $49 a month. Our Basic plan is $80.
  • You're a small crew that needs QuickBooks Online. Jobber includes it from Connect ($149 to $199 a month for 5 users). With us it's only on Enterprise ($300).
  • You need two-way texting with customers or job costing. Jobber has both on Grow.
  • You rely on Jobber's route optimization, GPS tracking, Xero sync or its 100+ app integrations. Jobber lists all of these.
  • You want a trial without entering a card. Jobber's trial doesn't ask for one. Ours does, and charges nothing until it ends.

How to switch from Jobber

  1. Start your free trial. You get 14 days, and nothing is charged until the trial ends.
  2. Bring your customers over. Export your customer list from Jobber as a CSV file and import it into Quorra Dispatch. On Enterprise, we help with the import and set up an onboarding call.
  3. Run a week of real jobs on both. Quote, book and get paid in Quorra while Jobber is still there if you need it.
  4. Then cancel Jobber. Check your Jobber plan first: on a 1-year commitment you keep paying until the 12 months are up, and a yearly prepaid plan isn't refunded.

Questions

Is there a cheaper alternative to Jobber for a small crew?

It depends on your size. For one person, Jobber Core is cheaper than our Basic plan. For a crew of up to 5 on a month-to-month plan, Quorra Essentials costs $1,995 in the first year, against $2,388 for Jobber Connect with no commitment.

How much does Jobber cost for 5 users?

As of November 12, 2026, Jobber Connect for 5 users is $199 a month with no commitment, $169 a month with a 1-year commitment, or $149 a month billed yearly. Grow for 5 users is $299, $259 or $229. Extra users are $29 a month each.

Can I move my customers over from Jobber?

Yes. Export them from Jobber as a CSV and import the file into Quorra Dispatch. The Enterprise plan includes data import help and an onboarding call.

Where do card payments go?

Straight into your own Stripe account. Quorra Dispatch doesn't hold your money.

Do I need a contract, and can I cancel?

No contract. Pay monthly (with 50% off your first 3 months) or yearly (save 10%), and cancel any time from the Billing page in the app. You keep access until the end of the period you already paid for.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Quorra Dispatch is an iPhone and Android app that also works in the browser. Your crew uses the same app with their own logins.
