Frequently asked questions

How is Quorra Dispatch different from Jobber?

Quorra Dispatch is priced by number of logins ($80, $190, or $300 a month) and is built for contractors and field crews. Check Jobber's own site for its current plans and pricing so you can compare side by side.

Can I move my customers over from Jobber?

Yes. CSV imports are supported, and the Enterprise plan includes data import help and an onboarding call.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every plan starts with a 14-day free trial.
